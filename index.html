SoftBloom Wiki

SoftBloom is built around steady progress: learn the basics, grow your skills, earn money, climb ranks, then take on the dungeons and bosses. Go at your own pace. This guide gives you a path, and you can always wander off and explore.

Quick start
  1. Register with /register <password> <password>.
  2. Open /kit and claim the Starter kit and the Claim kit.
  3. Mark your land with the Golden Shovel from the Claim kit. You start with 100 claim blocks and can claim 2 separate lands.
  4. Set a home with /sethome. Come back any time with /home.
  5. Start earning: browse /quest, and sell what you collect at /warp shop.

Stage 1: Settle In covers all of this in more detail. Click any command on this wiki to copy it.

Need a command fast? Open the Command Cheat Sheet. Want to meet the community? See Community for Discord, social media and the store.

1. Settle In →

Stage 1: Settle In (your first hour)

Register

  • First time here? Type /register <password> <password>.
  • Next time you join, type /login <password>. You only need this if the password window doesn’t pop up on its own.

Get started

  • Claim your Starter kit from the Kits GUI (/kit). It has enchanted stone tools, basic armor, food and a backpack, and you can claim it once. See the Kits page.
  • Grab the Claim kit too. It has a Golden Shovel and the Claim Guide book. There’s also a Guide kit with the Guide Book.
  • Set a home with /sethome and return with /home. /back returns you to where you last were, including where you died.
  • Ask a friend for a teleport with /tpa <player>.
  • Type /warp to see the warps: shop, crates, pvp, parkour, church, blackjack, community chest, pinata, pirate koth and afk haven.
  • Type /rtp to open a travel menu that teleports you to a random spot in the Overworld, Resourceworld, Nether or Limbo.
  • Type /translator to chat across languages. Choose your Incoming messages (what others write, translated for you) and your Outgoing messages (what you write, translated for others).
  • Type /discord to get the Discord link.
  • Playing on Bedrock? Everything in this guide works for you too. A few custom items may look slightly different from Java.

Claiming land

  • You start with 100 claim blocks.
  • Use the Golden Shovel to mark two corners of your claim. Everything inside is protected.
  • You can claim 2 separate lands. Both can be expanded as far as your claim blocks allow.
  • You earn 50 to 100 free claim blocks every hour you play.
  • Every rank-up gives you a big bonus of claim blocks on top (see the rank table).
  • The Claim Guide book in your Claim kit shows you how it works.

Stage 2: Skills

Everything you do trains a skill. There are 11 skills: Fighting, Agility, Farming, Alchemy, Mining, Foraging, Excavation, Fishing, Enchanting, Archery and Defense.

  • Open the skill menu with /skill:skill.
  • Each skill has milestone rewards you claim once from its menu.
  • Skills are also your rank requirement. Each rank asks for a minimum level in all skills, so you can’t just grind one.
  • You don’t need to pick a skill to level it. Anything that gives XP for a skill levels that skill automatically, and one activity can level several skills at once. For example, killing mobs while mining raises both Fighting and Mining.
  • Tip: just play how you like. Check the skill menu now and then to see which skills are behind, then do something that gives them XP.

Stage 3: Making Money

  • Sell at /warp shop. Ores, mob drops, crops, ocean items and blocks all sell.
  • Even More Fishing: use /emf toggle to switch between vanilla fishing and the Even More Fishing plugin, which adds custom fish of different rarities. Sell your catch at /emf shop: the rarer the fish, the higher the price.
  • Spawners cost between $3,000 and $450,000. Start with the cheapest and upgrade as you earn.
Heads upLimit: 4 spawners per chunk. If you try to place more, you’ll get a message.
  • Rank-ups cost nothing. They give you free money, claim blocks and fly time.
  • Free money: chat games, fishing contests, quests (/quest), crates and your daily calendar all pay out.
  • Quests: type /quest to browse over 1,600 quests. Each one asks you to do something you’d do anyway and pays money when you finish it. Most come in tiers: finish one to unlock the next, harder one, and each quest can be completed once.
    • Categories: mining, digging, woodcutting, farming, fishing, hunter, crafting, smelting, brewing, enchanting, building, collecting, weaponsmith, activities (milking, shearing, taming, breeding, trading and bartering) and misc (walking and playtime).
    • EXP quests pay XP instead of money, and Impossible quests are huge goals with the biggest rewards.

Stage 4: Ranks

Your rank climbs through 10 steps, from Member to Eternal. To rank up you need playtime and a minimum level in every skill. There is no fee: each rank-up pays you free money, claim blocks and fly time as an instant reward.

How to rank up

Not sure how far along you are? Try the Rank Calculator.

  • /ranks shows your rank progress.
  • /rankup shows your requirements. If something is missing, it tells you what.
  • When you meet every requirement, /rankup opens a window where you accept the rank-up.

Every rank-up unlocks a new dungeon and its warp, and more pet storage (see Stage 5).

Heads upMembers can’t equip an elytra. Reach Settler and you can.

Daily Reward

Play 60 active minutes in a calendar day to unlock your daily calendar reward. Claim it with /calendar. “Active” means you’re actually playing: the server watches whether you look around, not whether you stand still. The AFK zone won’t count.

Voting

Type /vote to see the voting links, or /voteall to see every site. Votes give you crate keys and rewards, and they fill the piñata bar. When it’s full, a piñata spawns and everyone online can hit it.

Premium ranks

Premium ranks are a separate ladder that sits alongside your rank. Their perks are listed in the Discord OG Shop and in the SoftBloom online store. See the Premium Ranks page for every perk.

Stage 5: Gear and Enchants

Custom enchants

  • There are 173 custom enchants in six tiers: Simple, Unique, Elite, Ultimate, Fabled and Legendary.
  • Open /enchanter to buy enchantment books. Right-click a tier’s book to get a random enchant from that tier.
  • Drag and drop a book onto your item. Each book shows its success and destroy rate.
  • Items have 2 custom enchant slots. A slot increaser adds up to 6 more.
  • A White Scroll protects an item from being destroyed on a failed book.
  • The Warp Shop (/warp shop) sells slot increasers, White Scrolls and other custom enchant items.
  • Use /tinkerer to trade unwanted enchants for useful items and /alchemist to combine books.
WarningGrindstones remove custom enchants, so don’t use one on an item you want to keep.

Crates and keys

  • Basic, Advanced and Vote crates are open to everyone, each dungeon has its own crate inside the dungeon, and events have their own crates.
  • Keys come from fishing contests, chat trivia, voting, dungeons and events.
  • Some crate rewards are vouchers that you redeem once, such as fly time and claim blocks.

Pets

Level a pet and choose a skill tree: Combat, Farm, PvP, Ranger, Ride or Utility. Ride pets are fast mounts, Farm and Utility pets pick up items and carry an inventory, and Combat or PvP pets help in a fight. Your pet storage grows as you rank up. See the Pets page for every skill tree.

Rings

Wearable rings give permanent bonuses while they’re in your inventory. Stronger gems and golden rings are better, and Sapphire rings boost your skill XP. Rings stack, so you can wear several. Rings can only be obtained from premium crates.

Handy Tricks

  • Bottle your XP: hold a glass bottle and right-click an enchanting table. It costs 12 XP and gives you an XP bottle. To get your XP back in full, smelt the bottle in a furnace (12 XP plus your glass bottle). Throwing it returns only 3 to 11 XP.
  • Armored Elytra: drop an elytra and a chestplate on the same anvil and wait a second. They merge into one item that flies like an elytra and has the chestplate’s armor stats, with enchantments kept. Drop the merged item on a grindstone to split it back. If the elytra wears out, the item splits instead of breaking.
  • Budding amethyst: mine it with Silk Touch and it drops as a block.
WarningAnvil: there’s no “Too Expensive” limit, so check the cost before you click.
  • Villagers: trades restock instantly, up to 30 trades before a short cooldown.
  • Elevators: place Iron Blocks on top of each other. Jump on one to go up to the next iron block, and crouch to go down.
  • Player warps: you can create up to 2 of your own.

How a dungeon works

  • Mobs spawn near their spawners when players are close. If you run too far, they leash back and heal.
  • Dungeon bosses spawn somewhere inside the dungeon, and a boss returns every 6 hours. Finding it is up to you.
  • Keys can be obtained from each dungeon’s bosses. Every boss drops a cosmetic key for a cosmetic that is exclusive to that dungeon.
  • Some normal dungeon mobs have a small chance to drop that dungeon’s key.
  • Every dungeon has its own crate inside the dungeon. Find it and use that dungeon’s keys there to get your reward. Example: Cursed Hollow keys open the crate inside Cursed Hollow.
  • Each dungeon has a trader inside it with decent trades. Bring the loot you collect there.
  • Dungeons drop trading materials specific to that tier (tier 7: squid tentacles, magic crystals, ectoplasm; tier 8: pig leather, magma gel, ghast eyes).
  • Bring food, potions and friends.

Suggested Path: First to Last

You can play in any order, but this is the path we recommend. Ranks are the milestones, and the Overworld is packed with things to find along the way, so keep exploring it at every stage.

Member to Settler: get set up

  • Claim your kits with /kit, claim land, and set /sethome.
  • Claim your /calendar reward and /vote every day.
  • Just play. Any activity that gives skill XP levels that skill, so reaching level 5 in every skill comes naturally.
  • Open /quest to get paid for things you’re already doing.
  • Sell at /warp shop and try /emf toggle for fishing money.
  • Use /rtp to explore the Overworld or gather materials in the Resourceworld.

Settler to Adventurer: dungeons 1 to 3

  • Do dungeons 1 to 3 and buy the cheapest spawners (4 per chunk).
  • Get your first enchant books and White Scrolls.
  • Join fishing contests and KOTH when they run.
  • Elytra unlocks at Settler, so you can fly up to Heaven.

Pathfinder to Elder: dungeons 4 to 6

  • Do dungeons 4 to 6.
  • Explore the Nether and Katter’s structures, then the Deep Blue.
  • Upgrade your spawners and gear.
  • Start chasing rings from premium crates.

Pioneer to Ancient: dungeons 7 and 8

  • Do dungeons 7 and 8.
  • Take on the Deeper Dark, because Wardens are dangerous.
  • Start The Sift if you haven’t yet (recommended from Veteran).
  • Try the first dragon tiers and visit Limbo.

Eternal: dungeon 9 and beyond

  • Do dungeon 9, the harder dragon tiers and Ominous fights, and collect the dragon gear and cosmetics.

FAQ

Quick answers to the questions new players ask most. Click a question to open it.

How do I join the server?

Java players connect to sftblmorg.usga.me:12006 on 1.21.11. Bedrock players use sftblmorg.usga.me with port 12006, on 1.21.11 or newer. The details are on the home page.

I just joined. What do I do first?

Type /register <password> <password>. Next time you join, use /login <password> if the password window doesn't appear. Then open /kit and claim your Starter kit and Claim kit. See Stage 1.

How do I protect my base?

Claim land with the Golden Shovel from the Claim kit. Mark two corners and everything inside is protected. You start with 100 claim blocks, earn 50 to 100 more every hour you play, and get a bonus on every rank-up. You can claim 2 separate lands.

How do I rank up?

You need enough playtime and a minimum level in every skill. Type /rankup to see what is missing. Rank-ups are free and pay money, claim blocks and fly time. Use the Rank Calculator to see how close you are.

Why can't I equip an elytra?

Members can't equip an elytra. Reach the Settler rank (2 hours of playtime and level 5 in every skill) and you can.

How do I make money?

Sell resources at /warp shop, finish quests, join fishing contests, win chat games, claim your daily /calendar reward and vote with /vote. Check the Shop Prices to see what pays the most.

How do I get my daily reward?

Play 60 active minutes in a day, then type /calendar. Active means you are moving your camera. Standing in the AFK zone doesn't count.

How do I get to the dungeons?

Every rank-up unlocks the next dungeon. Open /warp and pick it. Go in order. See The 9 Dungeons.

How do I get keys?

Keys come from fishing contests, chat trivia, voting, dungeon mobs and dungeon bosses. Dungeon keys open the crate inside that dungeon, so find the crate there. Other keys are used at /warp crates.

How do I get flight?

Fly time comes from rank-ups, chat trivia and some crate vouchers. Climbing the ranks gives the most. Elytra work too, once you reach Settler.

How do I turn PvP on or off?

Type /pvp. PvP is off by default in the Overworld. Every other world is always PvP-on, so be careful.

How do I get home, to spawn or back to where I died?

/home goes to your home (set it with /sethome), /spawn goes to spawn and /back returns you to your last spot, including where you died.

How do I reach the Deeper Dark and the Deep Blue?

Use the portals at /spawn, in the portal areas. You can leave by typing /spawn, using /warp or teleporting to a home. Heaven is different: fly up past y=300 with an elytra.

Where did my items go?

Stray items and mobs are cleaned up about every 6 minutes, so don't leave anything valuable on the ground. Store loot in chests and keep your claim protected.

How many spawners can I place?

Four per chunk. If you try to place more, you'll get a message.

Can I play on Bedrock?

Yes. Everything works on Bedrock. A few custom items may look slightly different from Java.

Where do I get rings?

Rings come only from premium crates. See Stage 5.

What do premium ranks give?

Premium perks are listed in the Discord OG Shop and the SoftBloom online store. Prices, perks and keys for every rank are on the Premium Ranks page.

Where can I get help?

Type /discord or join the SoftBloom Discord and ask. Staff and players are happy to help.

The rank ladder

Your rank is marked You. A rank marked Ready is one you can claim now, and Next is the one to aim for.

Your playtime and skill levels come from /rankup and /skill:skill. Rank-ups cost nothing. They pay free money, claim blocks and fly time.
